 Solnix keeps its package cache fresh as a community. Your machine can
 help: build queued packages in idle time, seed what it has fetched to
 other users, and report its platform (architecture + illumos flavour,
 resolved to a country and then forgotten — never your IP or a machine
 ID) so we know where to aim effort.

 This is OFF until you choose it. To join, set in your configuration:

     solnix.privacy.enable = false;   # opt into the community cache

 To go further and become a trusted builder that adds to the cache, see
 the handbook — it needs your host's SSH identity and (optionally) a
 contact address:
     https://solnix.io/docs/handbook/keeping-solnix-fresh.html
